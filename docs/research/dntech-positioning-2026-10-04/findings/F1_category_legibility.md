# F1 — “Software house” wins category legibility; “product engineering studio” wins differentiation

## Thesis

Use **software house / software development** as the category descriptor for Indonesian discoverability and buyer comprehension. Keep **product engineering studio** as the differentiating operating model, not the only label.

## Evidence

- DN Tech already uses both: “Product engineering studio” in homepage role copy and “Software Development Indonesia” / “software house” in SEO and FAQ language (sources 01–02).
- IBM, thoughtbot, and Thoughtworks attach product engineering to lifecycle work: design, validation, delivery, optimization, modernization, and continuous improvement (sources 03–05).
- Indonesian competitors lead with “software house” while offering custom software, internal systems, ERP, integrations, and support (sources 06–08).

## Confidence

Moderate-high. The evidence is strong for observed category language and positioning mechanics, but not for actual keyword volume or conversion lift.
