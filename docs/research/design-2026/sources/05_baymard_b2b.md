# Source 05 — B2B UX benchmark, Baymard Institute

- Type: independent benchmark/research library summary
- URL: https://baymard.com/ux-benchmark/collections/business-to-business
- Reviewed: 2026-09-26

## Verbatim excerpts

> “Each of the 27 B2B sites have been manually rated across 500 different e-commerce UX elements.”

> “Product evaluation was undermined by poor presentation — Sites often lacked the visuals, glossary support, and layout clarity necessary to make sense of dense product data ...”

## Interpretation

Even though DN Tech is not a traditional ecommerce catalog, the same evaluation problem exists on product pages: visitors need screenshots, plain-language feature explanations, use cases, status, pricing context, and a clear next step. “Product” should not mean a logo card with a paragraph.
