# F2 — The winning architecture is a hybrid, with explicit hierarchy

## Thesis

Recommended hierarchy:

> **DN Tech — software house untuk membangun produk dan workflow bisnis yang penting.**  
> **Product engineering studio untuk tim yang ingin scope jelas, delivery bertahap, dan software yang terus bisa dikembangkan.**

The first sentence answers “what are you?”; the second answers “how are you different?”.

## Why this is stronger than either/or

- A hard “studio” lead increases explanation cost for broad buyers.
- A hard “software house” lead risks being grouped with broad vendors that sell every stack and every project type.
- Local competitor evidence shows the two ideas already coexist; therefore the advantage is clarity of hierarchy and proof, not vocabulary novelty (sources 06–09).

## Required proof

To make the studio claim credible, show:

1. first-party products such as dnPeople as working proof;
2. discovery/scope/roadmap artifacts;
3. product-quality delivery: testing, deployment, documentation, support;
4. named buying jobs and explicit “not a fit” boundaries;
5. case studies or honest status labels, not unsupported scale claims.

## Confidence

High as a strategic architecture; moderate for market outcome until tested.
