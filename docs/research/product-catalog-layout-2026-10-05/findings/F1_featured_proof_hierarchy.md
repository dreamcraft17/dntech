# F1 — Product catalogs need a featured proof hierarchy

## Thesis

The catalog should not give every product equal visual weight. A featured product hero followed by a compact product index makes the page easier to scan and gives DN Tech one place to explain why its products are credible.

## Evidence

i10Studios, thoughtbot, Looplogic, Orion, and DARN all expose a featured work/product or product-story hierarchy before the longer list (sources 03–07). The existing DN Tech UX decision also identified the old grid as visually generic (source 01).

## Confidence

High for layout pattern; conversion impact still requires measurement.
