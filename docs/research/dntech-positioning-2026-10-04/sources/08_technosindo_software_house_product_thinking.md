# Source 08 — Technosindo: software house with product-thinking language

- Type: primary / public competitor evidence
- URL: https://technosindo.com/
- Credibility: high for observed Indonesian competitor messaging; vendor bias
- Recency: high

## Evidence

Technosindo leads with “Software House Indonesia” while also claiming product thinking, UI/UX, software engineering, discovery, roadmap, maintenance, iteration, optimization, and end-to-end development for web, mobile, internal systems, and integrations.

## Short verbatim excerpts

> “Software House Indonesia”

> “Kami menggabungkan product thinking, UI/UX, software engineering, dan delivery process”

> “Plan … Build … Scale”

## Interpretation

This is the strongest counterexample to a hard either/or choice. A software-house label can still communicate product thinking. The strategic opportunity for DN Tech is to own a more specific version of that hybrid, not to assume the category label itself creates differentiation.
