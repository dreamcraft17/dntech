- Type: Government service guidance
- Publisher: Government Digital Service
- Accessed: 2026-09-22
- Scores: credibility 5/5, recency 4/5, bias 4/5

## Relevant evidence

The guidance recommends descriptive headings and link text that remains meaningful when screen-reader users encounter links outside their visual context.

Short excerpt: “Break up long pages with headings. It’s easier to scan and read.”

## Design implication

Use a direct question as the section heading and keep every service title visible. Preserve semantic headings and meaningful destination context.
