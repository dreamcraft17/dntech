# Refresh targets

- Product detail click-through rate from featured hero vs supporting index.
- Scroll depth to price, use cases, and FAQ by product slug/device.
- Product filter usage and zero-result rate.
- Five-user moderated task: identify fit, launch status, price signal, and next step.
- Qualified demo/contact starts from product pages.
- Search Console landing queries for HRIS, ERP, operational software, workflow, and product names.
- Review whether “featured” changes perceived product maturity or creates confusion.

## Success thresholds for the first review

- ≥80% of moderated users can identify a suitable product and next step without explanation.
- No critical accessibility issue in keyboard/screen-reader pass.
- Mobile LCP target ≤2.0s p75 for the first viewport.
- No increase in product-page error or zero-result rate.
