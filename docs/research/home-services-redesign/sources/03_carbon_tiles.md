- Accessed: 2026-09-22

## Relevant evidence

Carbon supports tiles for short service information and navigation, but recommends clear left alignment, flat hierarchy, and interaction behavior that matches whether the entire tile or only an internal action is clickable.

Short excerpt: “Tiles reside in the same plane as the background layer—they do not have elevation.”

## Design implication

Cards are not inherently wrong. For DN Tech, a flat, border-separated service index provides the needed grouping without shadows, decorative elevation, or repeated card chrome.
