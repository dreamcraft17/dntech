# F3 — Adversarial review

## Challenge 1: Does the preview fabricate proof?

It could if it showed fake metrics or an actual product screenshot that is not available. The implementation uses a clearly labelled “workflow preview” with abstract bars and copy about building/testing/iterating; it does not claim customer counts or business impact.

## Challenge 2: Is one featured product unfair?

The featured choice follows the existing CMS `featured` field and every other returned product remains visible in the supporting index. The page hierarchy is not a ranking claim.

## Challenge 3: Is the layout too dense on mobile?

The hero columns stack, the supporting cards remain readable, and the page keeps the existing category navigation. Follow-up validation should test task completion, not aesthetic preference.

## Challenge 4: Does this overfit famous studio websites?

The pattern is abstracted to hierarchy and decision cues, not copied visually or textually. DN Tech keeps its navy/teal/orange system and CMS-driven content.
