# Refresh targets

Review monthly or after every 10 qualified conversations:

- Category terms in inbound leads: software house, software development, app development, MVP, product engineering, integrasi, ERP, HR/payroll.
- Qualified-conversation rate by landing-page route: MVP/product, workflow, integration/modernization, product demo.
- Search Console impressions/clicks for category and job terms.
- Win/loss reasons mentioning trust, price, scope clarity, technical depth, or category fit.
- DN Tech proof inventory: first-party product status, public case studies, testimonials, demos, delivery artifacts.
- Competitor language from Embrace ID, NIS Group, Technosindo, Nagatama, and new Indonesian SERP entrants.

## Refresh trigger

Reconsider the hierarchy if, over a minimum of 20 qualified conversations, one category phrase shows a materially higher qualified rate or if interview comprehension for the hybrid message falls below 4 of 5 buyers.
