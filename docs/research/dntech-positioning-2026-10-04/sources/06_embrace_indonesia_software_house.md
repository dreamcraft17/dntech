# Source 06 — Embrace ID: Indonesian software-house positioning

- Type: primary / public competitor evidence
- URL: https://embrace.id/en
- Credibility: high for observed competitor messaging; vendor bias
- Recency: high

## Evidence

Embrace leads with “AI-Powered Software House” and states that it builds custom software for every business need, including web apps, e-commerce, internal systems, and ERP. Its page combines the broad category with senior engineers, transparent process, discovery, design, build, deploy, support, and quantified proof claims.

## Short verbatim excerpts

> “One software house in Indonesia for all your software needs.”

> “we design and build whatever application your business needs”

> “Discovery … Design … Build … Deploy … Support”

## Interpretation

“Software house” is legible and can carry a full-funnel offer, but competitors already occupy the broad promise. DN Tech would need a sharper wedge and more distinctive proof to avoid being compared mainly on price, stack, and delivery speed.
