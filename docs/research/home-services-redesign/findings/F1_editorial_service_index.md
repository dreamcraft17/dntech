# F1 — An editorial service index fits this decision better than equal cards

Status: supported, with directional confidence

## Thesis

Visitors evaluating DN Tech need to compare service names and descriptions. A flat, vertically ordered index makes that comparison easier than equal cards with decorative numbers and repeated CTA labels.

## Evidence chain

- NN/g: the label, supporting content, and context jointly create information scent.
- GOV.UK: descriptive headings and links improve scanning and out-of-context comprehension.
- Carbon: service tiles can remain flat and should not gain elevation by default.
- WCAG 2.2: large targets, visible focus, and meaningful order support mobile and keyboard use.

## Product implication

The section's job is not to showcase four visual objects. Its job is to help a prospective client recognize their problem and choose a credible next path.

## Limits

No DN Tech session recordings, click data, or user interviews were available. The direction is grounded in established guidance and code evidence, but conversion impact still needs behavioral measurement.
