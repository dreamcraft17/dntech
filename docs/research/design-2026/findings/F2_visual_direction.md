# F2 — Human specificity is the visual differentiator

## Thesis

Use a restrained editorial system: navy/teal/orange tokens, generous white space, strong typography, real product screenshots, annotated workflow diagrams, actual team/founder photography, and short evidence captions. Avoid decorative 3D blobs, generic dashboard mockups, stock “team smiling” imagery, endless gradients, and motion as the primary source of polish.

## Evidence

- NN/G identifies design quality, disclosure, current content, and external connection as durable trust factors (source 04).
- The same research warns that generic photos are filler and that users value seeing the real process and people (source 04).
- Existing repo already includes product screenshots, media models, team/testimonial components, and content fields (source 01).

## Confidence

Medium-high. The direction is evidence-aligned; visual preference should still be tested with 5–8 target-user interviews or a preference/intent test.
