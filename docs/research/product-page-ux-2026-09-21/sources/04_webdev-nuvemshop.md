# Source 04 — web.dev: Nuvemshop image prioritization case study

URL: https://web.dev/case-studies/nuvemshop
Type: Primary platform case study
Accessed: 2026-09-21

## Relevant evidence

The case study reports that LCP and Core Web Vitals improvements were associated with better mobile engagement and conversion in the studied storefront cohort. It also warns against lazy-loading the top image and against applying priority to too many images.

Short quote: “if everything is high priority, nothing is.”

## Application to dntech

Keep the first viewport visually strong but light. Measure page performance and CTA engagement after launch; do not assume the UI treatment alone caused conversion changes.
