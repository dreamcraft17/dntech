# F4 — Anti-slop revision

## Decision

The product catalog should read like a working product index, not a generic SaaS landing page.

## Changes

- Removed the decorative glow layers and the pseudo-dashboard bars. DN Tech does not currently have product screenshots or approved usage metrics in this page context, so those visuals would imply evidence we do not have.
- Replaced the generic preview panel with a workflow rail built from each product's real feature teasers. The numbered rail gives visitors a quick answer to “pekerjaan apa yang dibantu produk ini?” without inventing UI.
- Replaced the repeated card grid with a ruled product index. The list keeps comparison easy across product name, category, description, availability, price, and next step.
- Kept one strong signature surface: the navy featured-product panel paired with the pale workflow rail. Its contrast has a job—separating the primary recommendation from the product index—not decoration.
- Kept the copy and status labels tied to the existing product schema and localization files.

## Anti-slop check

| Dimension | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Product/user/task fit | 2 — helps owners, HR, and operations leads identify the closest workflow product and open its detail page |
| Context/brand fit | 2 — practical, engineering-led, Indonesian B2B tone; no unverifiable claims |
| Specificity | 2 — workflow rail uses actual feature teaser data and the index exposes real catalog fields |
| Restraint | 2 — no glow, fake charts, glassmorphism, or repeated elevated cards |
| Signature detail | 2 — numbered workflow rail is distinctive and content-driven |
| Deliberate craft | 2 — asymmetry, ruled index, and contrast establish hierarchy and scan order |

Total: 12/12.
