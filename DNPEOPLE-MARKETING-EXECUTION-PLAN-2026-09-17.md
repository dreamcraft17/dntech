## 1. Mission

Membawa dnPeople ke pipeline customer awal dengan positioning yang tajam dan dapat dibuktikan:

> **Aturan HR pusat, tetap rapi di setiap cabang.**

DN Tech memasarkan dnPeople sebagai solusi untuk branch operations, payroll confidence, dan people evidence—bukan sekadar “HRIS all-in-one” atau produk absensi murah.

## 3. Campaign architecture

### Campaign A — Branch Operations Control

**Audience:** owner, HR/GA, operations manager perusahaan multi-lokasi.  
**Pain:** aturan cabang berbeda, attendance exception menumpuk, approval WhatsApp/Excel.  
**Offer:** `Workflow Audit Cabang 30 Menit`.  
**Proof:** scope organisasi, lokasi, shift, approval, correction evidence, import dry-run.  
**CTA:** `Audit workflow cabang Anda`.

### Campaign B — Payroll Confidence

**Audience:** finance manager, HR payroll, owner.  
**Pain:** payroll dibuka ulang, input attendance/OT tidak terlacak, sulit menjelaskan angka.  
**Offer:** `Payroll Evidence Checklist` + guided demo.  
**Proof:** BPJS/PPh 21, proration, preview, finalize, payslip verification.  
**CTA:** `Lihat alur payroll yang dapat diperiksa`.

### Campaign C — People Evidence

**Audience:** HR lead, compliance-minded owner, growing company.  
**Pain:** tidak ada kronologi keputusan, dokumen/policy acknowledgement tercecer.  
**Offer:** `HR Evidence Readiness Review`.  
**Proof:** before/after, actor, reason, approval, audit export, privacy controls.  
**CTA:** `Review kesiapan evidence HR`.

Campaign C dimulai setelah Campaign A/B menghasilkan percakapan nyata; jangan meluncurkan tiga narasi sekaligus tanpa attribution.

## 4. Channel playbook

### Owned: DN Tech website

Implementasikan halaman produk dnPeople di CMS/site DN Tech:

- product overview dengan tiga outcome pillars;
- use-case sections untuk branch operations/payroll/evidence;
- pricing orientation yang mengarah ke `hris.dntech.id` atau demo;
- CTA dengan UTM dan lead source;
- case study hanya dengan izin dan data nyata;
- blog yang menjawab pain, bukan artikel SEO generik.

Page `/products/dnpeople` harus mengarahkan ke satu CTA utama. Jangan membuat pengunjung memilih antara terlalu banyak menu.

### Founder-led outbound

Minggu 1–4: pilih 50 akun ICP, cari trigger (cabang baru, hiring HR/payroll, ekspansi, audit, migrasi software), kirim pesan personal, dan tawarkan workflow audit. Catat competitor/status quo, bukan hanya “interested”.

### Partnerships

Bangun referral motion dengan:

- payroll/tax consultant;
- implementer accounting/ERP;
- komunitas HR Indonesia;
- konsultan operasional retail/F&B;
- system integrator SME.

Partner diberi one-pager, qualification checklist, demo script, dan aturan klaim fitur.

### Content

Cadence awal:

- 1 deep article/minggu berbasis pain;
- 2 short posts/minggu dengan workflow/lesson;
- 1 small webinar/bulan;
- 1 anonymized proof/case study setelah ada izin.

Topik awal: audit attendance exception, payroll close checklist, approval cabang, dan migrasi Excel tanpa kehilangan evidence.

### Paid acquisition

Tunda broad paid ads sampai ada sinyal conversion dari outbound/partner. Uji paid search secara sempit pada intent:

- `absensi multi cabang`;
- `software HR perusahaan cabang`;
- `payroll Indonesia audit`;
- `approval HR cabang`.

Budget dan CAC target tetap TBD sampai baseline qualified conversion tersedia.

## 5. Funnel operating model

```text
Content / outbound / partner
          ↓
Qualified workflow audit
          ↓
Guided demo with prospect data
          ↓
Pilot / sandbox with success metric
          ↓
Payroll or approval milestone
          ↓
Paid conversion + permissioned proof
```

### Qualification fields

- employee count and growth;
- number of branches/locations;
- attendance and payroll current tools;
- exception frequency and last incident;
- approval owner;
- go-live deadline;
- must-have integration/native app/auto-transfer requirements;
- budget owner and decision process.

### Disqualifiers

- native app only;
- automatic bank transfer/e-filing is non-negotiable;
- customer expects unsupported Roadmap feature;
- no internal implementation owner;
- headcount/scope exceeds tier without Enterprise discussion.

## 7. Competitive talk track

Kompetitor seperti Mekari Talenta, Gadjian, dan GreatDay HR kuat pada HRIS generik: payroll, attendance, leave, self-service, performance, dan recruitment. Respons DN Tech:

- akui area yang memang kuat;
- jangan membuat klaim kelemahan tanpa verifikasi;
- bawa percakapan ke workflow exception dan evidence;
- demo berdasarkan kasus prospek;
- gunakan spreadsheet/WhatsApp sebagai status quo yang dapat diukur;
- jangan menang hanya dengan harga.

## 10. Guardrails

Tidak boleh dipakai tanpa bukti:

- “ratusan perusahaan”;
- “5000+ karyawan”;
- “hemat 80%”;
- customer logo/testimonial tanpa izin;
- “auto-transfer payroll”;
- “e-filing DJP/BPJS”;
- native mobile;
- AI selalu akurat;
- fitur Conditional sebagai live tanpa syarat.

Semua materi harus menyebutkan bila fitur bergantung pada SMTP, biometric provider, SSO/IdP, LLM, payment live, atau UAT customer.
