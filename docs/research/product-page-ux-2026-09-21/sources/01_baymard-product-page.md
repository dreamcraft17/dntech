# Source 01 — Baymard Product Details Page UX Research Studies

URL: https://baymard.com/research/product-page
Type: Industry UX research
Accessed: 2026-09-21

## Relevant evidence

Baymard describes product-page research covering product layout, product descriptions, product video, FAQs, plan matrices, and cross-navigation. It specifically lists “One Long Page” and “Collapsed Sections” among product page layout patterns.

Short quote: “How users engage with and rely on product information and descriptions.”

## Application to dntech

Use a structured long page with visible headings and scannable cards. Product video is an optional research topic, not a requirement; the page should work without a tutorial/video dependency.
