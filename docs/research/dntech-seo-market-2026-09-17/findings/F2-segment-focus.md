# F2 — Concrete problem segments are more defensible than generic custom software

## Finding

The current catalog supports multiple service segments, but generic “developer murah/bisa bikin apa saja” messaging has low differentiation. The strongest initial segments are MVP/product engineering, complex multi-branch workflows, and legacy integration/modernization.

## Method

Scored with `segmentation_scorer.py --profile services`. The tool weights substantiality and accessibility most heavily and requires both gates to pass. Scores are assumptions, not observed conversion data.

## Decision implication

Build/test one page and one CTA per problem segment. Use dnPeople as a concrete first-party product proof, not as evidence that every DN Tech service has the same audience.
