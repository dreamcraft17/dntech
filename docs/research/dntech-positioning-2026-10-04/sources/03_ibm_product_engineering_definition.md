# Source 03 — IBM: product engineering definition and lifecycle

- Type: secondary / industry authority
- URL: https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/product-engineering
- Credibility: high
- Recency: medium-high
- Bias: vendor perspective

## Evidence

IBM defines product engineering as applying engineering principles across a product lifecycle, including design, development, testing, and optimization. It contrasts product engineering with the broader product-development process, which includes non-technical activities such as marketing and sales.

## Short verbatim excerpts

> “design, development, testing and optimization”

> “an ongoing process rather than an end state”

## Interpretation

The term carries a lifecycle and quality/iteration expectation. It is stronger than a synonym for “coding project”, but it may require explanation for buyers who are primarily seeking internal software or a website.
