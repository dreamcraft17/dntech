## Evidence

The audit identifies three strongest segments for validation: startup/product engineering, complex multi-branch workflows, and legacy integration/modernization. It also labels generic “developer murah” demand as a watch segment because differentiation is weak.

## Short verbatim excerpt

> “DN Tech should focus SEO and sales content around concrete buying situations rather than a broad custom-development promise.”

## Interpretation

This supports a problem-led positioning. It does not prove that the market prefers the words “product engineering studio”; it supports using named buying situations as the bridge between category language and differentiation.
