# Source 07 — NIS Group: local software-house category language

- Type: primary / public competitor evidence
- URL: https://nisgroup.id/jasa/jasa-software-house
- Credibility: high for observed Indonesian buyer language; vendor bias
- Recency: high

## Evidence

NIS Group uses “software house” as the page category and describes custom software, websites, mobile apps, ERP, dashboards, APIs, discovery, scope, roadmap, launch, documentation, and maintenance. It also targets a broad set of business types, from B2B services and manufacturing to retail and startups.

## Short verbatim excerpts

> “Jasa Software House untuk Website, ERP, Aplikasi, dan Sistem Bisnis”

> “tidak hanya menulis kode”

> “scope yang jelas”

## Interpretation

The local category is not inherently low quality; serious software houses also sell business understanding, clear scope, and lifecycle support. The distinction therefore cannot be “software house = bad, studio = good”. It must be made through target buyer, workflow, proof, and engagement model.
