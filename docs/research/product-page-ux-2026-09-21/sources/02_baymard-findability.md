# Source 02 — Baymard: Avoid Horizontal Tabs

URL: https://baymard.com/research-articles/avoid-horizontal-tabs
Type: Industry UX research
Accessed: 2026-09-21

## Relevant evidence

Baymard reports that horizontal tabs caused findability issues across B2B and consumer contexts, especially when important information was hidden in a tab.

Short quote: “reduced findability of content held within ‘Horizontal Tabs’”

## Application to dntech

Do not replace the current long product detail with hidden primary tabs. Keep the core sections visible in the page flow, with a compact anchor navigation only as a convenience.
