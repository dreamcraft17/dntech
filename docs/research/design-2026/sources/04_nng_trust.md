# Source 04 — Trustworthiness in Web Design, Nielsen Norman Group

- Type: independent UX research summary
- URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/
- Reviewed: 2026-09-26

## Verbatim excerpts

> “The 4 factors of trustworthiness are ... design quality, up-front disclosure, comprehensive and current content, and connection to the rest of the web.”

> “Meaningful navigation labels indicate that the company considers users’ needs and understands their mental models and vocabulary.”

> “Users ... hate if they have to fill in lengthy forms to obtain that information.”

> “Generic photos ... are more likely to be considered filler images.”

## Interpretation

The anti-AI-slop direction is not a style preference alone. It is a trust strategy: use meaningful labels, disclose commercial realities early, publish current and complete content, and replace filler imagery with actual work, people, and artifacts.
