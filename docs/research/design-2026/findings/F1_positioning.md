# F1 — Design around three buying jobs, not one generic agency funnel

## Thesis

DN Tech should organize the public experience around three jobs:

1. **Ship a product or MVP** — needs scope, milestones, technical judgment, and delivery proof.
2. **Repair a workflow across teams/branches** — needs process mapping, integration, approvals, and operational proof.
3. **Evaluate a first-party product** — needs fit, status, use cases, pricing, demo, and product evidence.

## Evidence

- Current site copy already names startups, business workflows, products, price, timeline, and support (sources 01–02).
- B2B research says buyers need enough information to research and shortlist before submitting contact data (source 03).
- Independent trust research favors meaningful navigation labels and upfront disclosure (source 04).

## Confidence

High for a design hypothesis; market fit still needs lead-quality and interview validation.
