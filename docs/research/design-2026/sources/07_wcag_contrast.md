# Source 07 — WCAG 2.2 Contrast Minimum, W3C

- Type: primary accessibility standard guidance
- URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html
- Reviewed: 2026-09-26

## Verbatim excerpts

> “The visual presentation of text ... has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.”

> “The inability to distinguish certain shades of color does not negatively affect contrast perception ... contrast is calculated ... independent of color perception.”

## Interpretation

The blue/teal/orange palette can remain, but it cannot rely on hue alone for hierarchy or status. Body text and interactive states need AA contrast, and status should include text/icon labels.
