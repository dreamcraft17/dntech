## Evidence

Looplogic uses a compact visual rhythm: method, proof, services, and shipped-product examples. Its message differentiates working product from mockups.

## Short quote

> “Most studios hand you mockups. We ship working product, faster.”

## Interpretation

A catalog benefits from a product-proof layer, but the proof should remain honest and tied to what the site can show.
