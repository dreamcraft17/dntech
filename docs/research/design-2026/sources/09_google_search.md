# Source 09 — Site names and snippets, Google Search Central

- Type: primary search documentation
- URL: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names
- Reviewed: 2026-09-26

## Verbatim excerpts

> “Choose a unique name that accurately reflects the identity of your site.”

> “Use your site name consistently across your home page.”

- Related source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet

## Interpretation

The visual and content system should use one canonical identity (“DN Tech”) consistently across header, footer, metadata, JSON-LD, product pages, and external references. Page-specific descriptions should be written for each intent, not generated from one generic agency sentence.
