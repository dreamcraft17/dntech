# Source 03 — B2B Usability, Nielsen Norman Group

- Type: independent UX research summary
- URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/b2b-usability/
- Reviewed: 2026-09-26

## Verbatim excerpts

> “You must establish a certain level of credibility before people are willing to give out their contact information.”

> “Many B2B sites ... fail to answer customers’ main questions or concerns.”

> “Users expect to get a basic understanding of products and services during their initial research ...”

## Interpretation

DN Tech should treat the site as a self-serve shortlist tool, not only as a lead form wrapper. Service and product pages need enough scope, price level, fit, process, and proof for a buyer to decide whether a conversation is worth having.
